About

I’m Mahnoor Ch., a passionate and creative Frontend Web Developer and UI/UX Designer from Okara, Punjab, Pakistan. With a B.Sc. in Computer Science from the University of the Punjab, I combine technical engineering knowledge with a strong sense of clean design to build modern, user-friendly applications. My ability to learn quickly, troubleshoot complex code patterns, and collaborate smoothly across dynamic tech teams helps me deliver high-quality production results.

I am always eager to adapt, grow, and take on challenging software engineering problems. Whether constructing scalable frontend architectures, customizing interactive portfolios, or designing intuitive digital products from scratch, I strive to create highly impactful online footprints.
